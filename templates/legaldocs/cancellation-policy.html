9. EU/UK Consumer Rights

If you are a consumer in the European Union or United Kingdom, you have the right to cancel most distance purchases within 14 days from the date you receive the goods, without giving any reason.

9.1 Right of Withdrawal

To exercise your right of withdrawal, inform us of your decision by a clear statement (e.g., email or letter).

9.2 Exceptions

The right of withdrawal does not apply to:

  • Sealed goods that are not suitable for return due to health or hygiene reasons once unsealed
  • Goods made to your specifications or clearly personalized
  • Sealed audio, video, or software once unsealed
  • Digital content once download has begun (with your consent)
  • Goods liable to deteriorate or expire rapidly
