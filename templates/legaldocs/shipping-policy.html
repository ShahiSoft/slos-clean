7. Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive:

  • Shipping confirmation email
  • Tracking number and carrier information
  • Link to track your package

You can also track your order by:

  • Logging into your account on our Website
  • Contacting customer service with your order number
  • Using the carrier's tracking website directly

Note: Tracking information may take 24-48 hours to update after shipment.

8. Delivery Information

8.1 Delivery Attempts

Carriers typically make {{delivery_attempts|default:"2-3"}} delivery attempts. After failed attempts, packages may be:

  • Left at a secure location (if authorized)
  • Held at a local carrier facility
  • Returned to sender

8.2 Signature Requirements

Signature may be required for:

  • High-value orders over {{signature_threshold|default:"$100"}}
  • Age-restricted products
  • Customer-requested signature confirmation

8.3 Delivery Instructions

You may provide special delivery instructions during checkout. We will attempt to accommodate requests, but cannot guarantee carrier compliance.

9. Shipping Issues

9.1 Lost Packages

If your package appears lost:

  1. Check tracking information for updates
  2. Verify the shipping address is correct
  3. Check with neighbors or building management
  4. Contact the carrier directly
  5. Contact us at {{support_email}}

We will investigate and, if confirmed lost, offer a replacement or refund.

9.2 Damaged Packages

If you receive a damaged package:

  1. Document the damage with photos
  2. Keep all original packaging
  3. Contact us within {{damage_report_window|default:"48 hours"}} of delivery
  4. We will arrange replacement or refund

9.3 Wrong Items or Missing Items

If you receive incorrect or incomplete orders:

  1. Contact us immediately at {{support_email}}
  2. Provide your order number and details of the issue
  3. We will ship correct items or issue appropriate credit

10. Address Changes

To change your shipping address:

  • Before processing: Contact us immediately - changes may be possible
  • After shipping: Contact the carrier directly to request redirect (fees may apply)

We are not responsible for packages delivered to incorrect addresses provided by the customer.

11. Special Circumstances

11.1 Holiday Shipping

During peak holiday seasons, please allow extra time for delivery. We recommend ordering early to ensure timely arrival.

11.2 Weather & Natural Disasters

Severe weather or natural disasters may cause delivery delays beyond our control. We appreciate your patience during these events.

11.3 COVID-19 & Health Emergencies

During health emergencies, delivery times may be affected. We prioritize the safety of our staff and delivery partners.
