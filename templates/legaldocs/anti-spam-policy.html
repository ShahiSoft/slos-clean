1. Introduction

{{company_legal_name}} ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to fighting spam and maintaining the integrity of our communications. This Anti-Spam Policy explains our practices regarding unsolicited electronic messages and outlines the standards we expect from users of our services.

2. What is Spam?

For the purposes of this policy, "spam" includes:

  • Unsolicited bulk email (UBE) - mass emails sent to recipients who haven't requested them
  • Unsolicited commercial email (UCE) - promotional emails sent without consent
  • Unsolicited messages through any electronic medium (SMS, social media, instant messaging)
  • Any communication that violates applicable anti-spam laws

3. Our Anti-Spam Commitment

We commit to the following practices:

3.1 Consent-Based Communications

  • We only send marketing emails to individuals who have explicitly opted in
  • We maintain accurate records of consent
  • We honor all opt-out requests promptly

3.2 Clear Identification

  • All our emails clearly identify {{company_legal_name}} as the sender
  • Our physical address is included in all commercial emails
  • Subject lines accurately reflect email content

3.3 Easy Unsubscribe

  • Every marketing email includes a clear unsubscribe link
  • Unsubscribe requests are processed within 10 business days
  • We do not require login or multiple steps to unsubscribe

4. Prohibited Activities

Users of our services are prohibited from:

  • Sending unsolicited bulk emails through our systems
  • Using our services to collect email addresses for spamming
  • Harvesting email addresses from our website or services
  • Sending emails with forged headers or deceptive subject lines
  • Using our name or brand in spam campaigns
  • Distributing malware, viruses, or harmful content via email
  • Sending chain letters or pyramid schemes
  • Engaging in email bombing or mail flooding
