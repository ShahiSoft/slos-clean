1. Our Commitment to Accessibility

{{company_legal_name}} is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards.

We strive to ensure that our website {{site_url}}{{if app_name}} and {{app_name}}{{/if}} are accessible to all users, regardless of ability or technology.

2. Conformance Standards

We aim to conform to the following accessibility standards:

  • Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA
  • Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act (for US visitors)
  • EN 301 549 (European accessibility standard)
  • Accessible Rich Internet Applications (ARIA) specifications

These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with disabilities and more user-friendly for everyone.

3. Accessibility Measures

{{company_legal_name}} takes the following measures to ensure accessibility:

  • Include accessibility as part of our mission statement
  • Integrate accessibility into our procurement practices
  • Provide continual accessibility training for our staff
  • Assign clear accessibility goals and responsibilities
  • Employ formal accessibility quality assurance methods
  • Include people with disabilities in our design and testing processes

4. Accessibility Features

Our website includes the following accessibility features:

4.1 Navigation

  • Consistent navigation throughout the site
  • Skip navigation links to bypass repetitive content
  • Logical heading structure
  • Descriptive link text
  • Keyboard-accessible menus and controls

4.2 Visual Design

  • Sufficient color contrast ratios
  • Resizable text without loss of functionality
  • No content that flashes more than three times per second
  • Alternative text for images
  • Visible focus indicators

4.3 Content

  • Clear and simple language
  • Captions and transcripts for multimedia content
  • Properly labeled form fields
  • Error identification and suggestions
  • Consistent identification of elements

4.4 Technical

  • Valid HTML and CSS
  • Proper use of ARIA landmarks and labels
  • Compatible with assistive technologies
  • Responsive design for various devices

5. Compatibility

Our website is designed to be compatible with the following assistive technologies:

  • Screen readers (JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver, TalkBack)
  • Screen magnification software
  • Speech recognition software
  • Alternative input devices

Our website is designed to work with the following browsers:

  • Latest versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge
  • Mobile browsers on iOS and Android

6. Known Limitations

Despite our best efforts, some content may not be fully accessible. Known limitations include:

  • Some older PDF documents may not be fully accessible
  • Third-party content or applications may have accessibility issues
  • Live video streams may not have real-time captions
  • Some interactive maps may require alternative navigation

We are actively working to address these limitations and improve accessibility.
