3. Age Verification Methods

We use the following methods to verify user ages:

3.1 Self-Declaration

Users may be asked to confirm their age through:

  • Date of birth entry during registration
  • Age confirmation checkboxes
  • Age gate pop-ups before accessing certain content

3.2 Third-Party Verification

For certain products or services, we may use third-party age verification services that verify age through:

  • Government-issued ID verification
  • Database verification against public records
  • Credit card verification (where card ownership implies minimum age)

3.3 In-Person Verification

For physical deliveries of age-restricted products, we may require:

  • ID check upon delivery
  • Signature from a person of legal age
  • Adult signature required designation

4. Parental Consent

4.1 COPPA Compliance (US Users)

For users under 13 years of age in the United States, we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.

4.2 Parental Controls

Parents or guardians may:

  • Request information about data collected from their child
  • Refuse further collection of their child's data
  • Request deletion of their child's personal information

4.3 Verifiable Parental Consent Methods

Where required, we obtain parental consent through:

  • Signed consent forms
  • Credit card verification with notification
  • Direct contact with parent via phone or video
  • Government ID verification of parent

6. User Responsibilities

By using our Website, you agree to:

  • Provide accurate age information
  • Not misrepresent your age to access age-restricted content

  • Supervise minors' use of the Website if you are their parent or guardian
  • Report suspected underage users accessing restricted content

7. Consequences of Violations

7.1 False Age Declaration

Providing false age information may result in:

  • Immediate account termination
  • Ban from future account creation
  • Cancellation of orders or purchases
  • Reporting to relevant authorities if required by law

7.2 Legal Consequences

False age declaration to purchase age-restricted products may be a criminal offense in many jurisdictions. You may be held legally responsible for any consequences.

8. Data Protection for Minors

We take additional precautions to protect minors' data:

  • No sale of minors' personal information
  • Age-appropriate privacy settings by default
  • Parental access to minors' account information
