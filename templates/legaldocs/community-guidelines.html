1. Introduction

Welcome to the {{company_legal_name}} community! These Community Guidelines establish the standards of behavior we expect from all members of our community on {{site_url}}{{if app_name}} and {{app_name}}{{/if}}.

Our goal is to create a safe, respectful, and inclusive environment where everyone can participate, share ideas, and engage positively with one another.

2. Core Principles

2.1 Respect

Treat all community members with respect and dignity. We embrace diversity and expect civil discourse even when disagreeing.

2.2 Safety

Help us maintain a safe environment. Report concerning behavior and never engage in activities that could harm others.

2.3 Authenticity

Be genuine and honest. Use accurate information and identify yourself authentically.

2.4 Legality

Follow all applicable laws and regulations. Do not use our platform for illegal activities.

3. Prohibited Content

The following content is not allowed on our platform:

3.1 Hate Speech and Discrimination

  • Content that promotes hatred based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or other protected characteristics
  • Slurs, stereotypes, or dehumanizing language
  • Support for hate groups or extremist ideologies

3.2 Harassment and Bullying

  • Personal attacks or targeted harassment of individuals
  • Cyberbullying or intimidation
  • Doxing (revealing personal information without consent)
  • Stalking behavior online or offline

3.3 Violence and Threats

  • Threats of violence against any person or group
  • Content that glorifies or promotes violence
  • Instructions for carrying out violent acts
  • Terrorism-related content

3.4 Harmful Content

  • Self-harm or suicide promotion
  • Content promoting dangerous activities
  • Graphic violence or gore
  • Content exploiting minors in any way

3.5 Adult Content

  • Sexually explicit material
  • Nudity (unless artistic and appropriately marked)
  • Sexual solicitation

3.6 Spam and Manipulation

  • Unsolicited commercial content or spam
  • Fake engagement (likes, follows, comments)
  • Misleading or clickbait content
  • Coordinated inauthentic behavior

3.7 Misinformation

  • Deliberately false or misleading information
  • Health misinformation that could cause harm
  • Manipulated media intended to deceive

3.8 Intellectual Property Violations

  • Copyright infringement
  • Trademark violations
  • Unauthorized use of others' content

4. Prohibited Behavior

The following behaviors are not permitted:

  • Impersonation: Pretending to be another person or organization
  • Account Abuse: Creating fake accounts or evading bans
  • Platform Manipulation: Using bots, scripts, or other tools to manipulate the platform
  • Doxxing: Sharing others' private information without consent
  • Brigading: Coordinated attacks on users or content
  • Scams: Fraudulent schemes or phishing attempts
  • Unauthorized Promotion: Advertising without permission

6. Content Best Practices

6.1 Quality Content

  • Create original, valuable content
  • Cite sources when sharing information
  • Use clear and appropriate language
  • Stay on topic in discussions

6.2 Constructive Engagement

  • Provide constructive feedback
  • Ask questions thoughtfully

7. Moderation

7.1 Our Approach

We use a combination of automated tools and human review to enforce these guidelines. Moderation decisions consider context, severity, and history.

7.2 Enforcement Actions

Violations may result in:

  • Warning: Notification of policy violation
  • Content Removal: Deletion of violating content
  • Temporary Suspension: Time-limited account restriction
  • Permanent Ban: Removal from the community
  • Legal Action: Reporting to authorities if required
