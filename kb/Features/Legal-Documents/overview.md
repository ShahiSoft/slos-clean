# Legal Documents Overview

## What is Legal Document Generator?

The Legal Document Generator creates professional, legally-compliant documents tailored to your business. Generate Privacy Policies, Terms of Service, Cookie Policies, and Accessibility Statements from customizable templates.

## Key Features

### Document Types

#### 1. Privacy Policy
- **Purpose:** Explain how you collect, use, and protect data
- **Required for:** GDPR (EU), CCPA (California), LGPD (Brazil)
- **Sections:** Data collection, usage, storage, rights, third-parties, retention, cookies
- **Auto-Generated:** Pulls from consent settings and company profile
- **Customizable:** Edit all sections before publishing

#### 2. Terms of Service
- **Purpose:** Define user obligations and liability
- **Sections:** User agreements, payment, disclaimers, limitation of liability
- **Legal Compliance:** GDPR compatible
- **Customizable:** All sections editable
- **Usage Rights:** Specify what users can/can't do with your service

#### 3. Cookie Policy
- **Purpose:** Explain cookie usage in detail
- **Cookie Inventory:** Auto-populated from scanner
- **Cookie Categories:** Essential, analytics, marketing, preferences
- **Purpose:** Explain what each cookie does
- **User Control:** Link to consent banner controls

#### 4. Accessibility Statement
- **Purpose:** Explain accessibility commitment
- **WCAG Reference:** Details WCAG 2.1 AA compliance status
- **Accessibility Measures:** Lists accessibility improvements made
- **Known Issues:** Discloses any remaining accessibility gaps
- **Contact:** Provides accessibility contact email

#### 5. Data Processing Agreement (DPA)
- **Purpose:** Document data processing arrangements
- **GDPR Requirement:** Required if you process personal data
- **Sections:** Scope, controller/processor roles, data protection
- **Customizable:** Edit terms and conditions
